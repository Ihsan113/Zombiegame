# Changelog

## 0.4.2 — tighter lane, grenades, loot drops and barricades
- Moved the play lane's top clamp from 56.5% to 52% of the screen and reduced the feet clearance to 22 px so the player/zombie ground point reaches just below the asphalt-side curb. The bottom boundary remains unchanged.
- Kept the starting grenade inventory at 3 and the touch grenade action; grenade arcs, detonations, area damage and synchronized effects remain included.
- Added labeled random loot crates for ammunition, grenades, wall kits and medkits; the loot loop starts in the first wave and subsequent supply crates arrive periodically.
- Added buildable, destructible timber barricades with HP; aligned zombies attack barricades before reaching players.
- Added six procedural WAV effects: grenade_throw, explosion, airdrop, wall_build, wall_break, and pickup.
- Android export version code/name: 15 / 0.4.2.

## 0.4.1 — lane bounds, wave clock, game over and SFX
- Added a per-wave countdown and overtime, squad-wide Game Over, clean restart, explicit save/continue, and eight original procedural WAV cues.

## 0.4.0 — arena and animation
- Added a more complete horror backdrop, lower-lane bounds, animated walk/attack/hurt visuals and horror-themed UI.
